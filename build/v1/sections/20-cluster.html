02Cluster

Three machines, one job at a time.

I run my own small cluster at home. Three machines on Tailscale. The laptop gives the orders. The PC with the RTX 4090 does the heavy work. The Mac mini takes the small MLX jobs.

What runs there

Video
MiniMax-H3, with up to 9 reference images and native audio. FastH3 Omni in int8, with Turbo and realism LoRAs stacked. LTX-2.5 in fp8: a 720p, 10 s clip in 94 s.
Images
FLUX-3 Klein, 5 to 10 s per 1024 px image.
Voices
VoxCPM.
Small LLMs
Qwen3-1.7B on llama.cpp. mlx-lm on the Mac mini.
Fine-tuning
LoRA fine-tuning with Unsloth in WSL.

Findings

Two renders at once ran 3.5x slower. So the GPU takes one job at a time.

The controller runs no models. It only sends jobs.
