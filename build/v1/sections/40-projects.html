    Home AI cluster and clip harness

    Three machines on Tailscale. A MacBook controls, an RTX 4090 PC does the heavy work, a Mac mini runs small MLX models. One GPU job at a time, because two renders at once ran 3.5x slower.

    It runs video diffusion (MiniMax-H3, LTX-2.5), FLUX images, VoxCPM voices and LoRA fine-tuning with Unsloth. LTX-2.5 renders a 10 s 720p clip in 94 s.

    The clip harness ties it together. A Qwen writer plans the scenes, FLUX draws keyframe sheets, H3 renders 10 to 20 s clips chained by their last frames, and agents score frame, voice and script from 0 to 100.
