about

Zaur Samadov

AI engineer · multimodal and LLM systems in production · experiencing GPU kernels and inference hardware

I am an AI engineer. I build multimodal and LLM systems for enterprise users and keep them running in production. The work covers the whole path: data and pipelines, model choice and fine-tuning, evaluation, guardrails and security, cost, deployment and on-call. I work closely with the people who use the systems.

I started with transformers in 2020 and keep learning. Right now I am studying inference hardware: CUDA kernels, profiling and serving, on my own GPU cluster. Work projects stay in-house; the public part is on Hugging Face and GitHub.
